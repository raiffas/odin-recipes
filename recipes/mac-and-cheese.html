Mac and Cheese Recipe

Description

Nothing is better than a big bowl of mac and cheese. Except a bowl that is homemade, creamy, and only needs 6 ingredients.

Ingredients

  • macaroni noodles
  • milk
  • butter
  • flour
  • white american kraft singles
  • seasoning to taste

Steps

  1. Cook your noodles, undercook them by 2 minutes
  2. Melt butter in a pan
  3. Once butter is melted, add flour and cook out the flour smell
  4. slowly mix in the milk
  5. Once desired consistency is reached, add cheese
  6. Mix in noodles and enjoy
