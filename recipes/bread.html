Bread Recipe

Description

Are you still buying bread? No more, here is how to make your own.

Ingredients

  • Bread flour
  • salt
  • sugar
  • yeast
  • water

Steps

  1. bloom the yeast in warm water with sugar
  2. in a bowl, mix the flour and salt
  3. once yeast has bloomed, add it to the the center of the flour bowl
  4. knead the dough
  5. let it rest, and come back 3 times to do coil folds every 30 minutes
  6. rest one more time and then turn it out onto baking sheet and bake in a 400 ° oven until brown
