Lasagna Recipe

Description

This recipe is for my world famous lasagna. It will be sure to delight your tastebuds and make your italian grandmother proud. Can you smell it already?

Ingredients

  • Lasagna Noodles
  • Pasta Sauce
  • Bechemel Sauce
  • Parsley
  • Mozzerella Cheese
  • Parmesan Cheese

Steps

  1. Precook the lasagna noodles. Undercook them by about 3 minutes
  2. Layer the lasagna by placing down tomato sauce, lasagna noodles, bechemel, and then cheese. Continue until all done.
  3. Bake at 400° until the top is brown and bubbly.
